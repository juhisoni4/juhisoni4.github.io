01

AI-Powered Energy Disaggregation for Virtual Sensing and Smart Monitoring

Generative AI · Time Series · Energy · Diffusion Models · Flow-based Models · VAE

Problem: Conventional monitoring systems depend on physical sensors that require installation and maintenance, making large-scale monitoring costly and vulnerable to sensor failures. This project explores energy disaggregation as a virtual sensing approach to estimate device-level energy consumption from aggregated electrical measurements.

Approach: Experimented with different deep learning and generative models, including Seq2Seq, Variational Autoencoder (VAE), Diffusion Model, and Flow-Based Model, to learn relationships between aggregate power signals and individual device consumption patterns. Evaluated model performance using RMSE, NDE, and SAE.

Results: The Flow-Based Model achieved the lowest RMSE, SAE, and NDE among the evaluated models. The estimated device-level energy signals demonstrate the potential of generative models for virtual sensing, continuous monitoring, energy optimization, and digital-twin applications. The proposed work is not limited to energy disaggregation and can be extended to other domains (ex. Healthcare, Manufacturing, etc.)

Python · PyTorch · Generative Models · Sklearn · Plotly · InfluxDB · Sequence Modeling · Nvidia DGX · Docker · MLflow

01

Steel Defect Segmentation using Deep Learning

Computer Vision · Semantic Segmentation · Industrial Quality Inspection

Problem: Manual steel surface inspection is time-consuming, inconsistent, and difficult to scale in industrial manufacturing.

Approach: Explored deep learning-based semantic segmentation for detecting and localizing steel surface defects at the pixel level. Experimented with and compared seven model configurations, including U-Net, U-Net++, FPN, DeepLabV3, and SegFormer with different backbone architectures.

Results: SegFormer with MiT-B2 achieved the best performance, with a macro Dice score of 0.6339 and macro IoU of 0.4772 after 200 epochs. Also analyzed model convergence, overfitting, and inference efficiency to evaluate the trade-offs between segmentation accuracy and computational performance.

Agentic Text-to-BQL: LLM- and RAG-Based Query Generation

LLM · Agentic AI · Automation

Developed an agentic AI system that translates natural language questions into Baja Query Language (BQL) queries, enabling users to access data without requiring technical knowledge of BQL syntax. The solution leverages Large Language Models (LLMs), Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), and an agentic workflow to understand user requests, retrieve relevant context, and generate appropriate BQL queries.

RESEARCH PAPER

Physics-Informed Diffusion Models for Unsupervised Anomaly Detection in Multivariate Time Series.

Juhi Soni, Markus Lange-Hegermann, Stefan Windmann, ECML Workshop on Learning from Small Data, 2025.

A novel approach for learning the physics-dependent temporal distribution of multivariate time-series data using a weighted physics-informed loss during diffusion model training. By incorporating physical knowledge into the training process, the diffusion model can more accurately capture the underlying data distribution, improving its ability to detect anomalies without labeled data. Experiments on both synthetic and real-world datasets demonstrate that physics-informed training improves anomaly detection performance, achieving higher F1 scores.

RESEARCH PAPER

Zero-shot facade parsing for generating building energy modeling inputs using vision-language models and generative image editing

Dennis Sprute, Juhi Soni, Gesa Benndorf, Energy & Buildings journal.

Building energy modeling (BEM) relies on accurate geometric and facade information to estimate energy demand and support building decarbonization strategies. However, extracting facade features such as windows from images remains challenging due to the need for annotated data, limited cross-domain generalization, and real-world occlusions. A zero-shot workflow proposed that combines vision-language-based semantic segmentation with generative image editing for occlusion compensation, requiring no task-specific fine-tuning. Evaluation results demonstrate improved segmentation performance and strong cross-dataset generalization, highlighting the approach's potential for scalable and transferable facade data extraction for BEM.
